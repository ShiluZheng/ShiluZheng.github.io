We are delighted to welcome Feixue Yu and Xuemin Gan to Zheng Lab. They both joined the team in 2025.

Feixue joined us as a PhD student. She is from Weihai and earned her master's degree from Inner Mongolia University. She is also a big fan of Pop Mart.

Xuemin joined us as a master's student. She is from Zhangzhou and earned her bachelor's degree from Fujian Agriculture and Forestry University. She is a big fan of Jiyi.
