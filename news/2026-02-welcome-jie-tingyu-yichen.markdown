We are delighted to welcome Jie Xie, Tingyu Bai, and Yichen Guo to Zheng Lab in Fall 2026.

Jie joined us as a PhD student. He is from Zunyi, Guizhou, and enjoys birding and playing basketball.

Tingyu joined us as a master's student. She is from Anshan, Liaoning, and is looking forward to her first field survey in the Zhangjiang Estuary.

Yichen, from Xuzhou, Jiangsu, joined us as a research assistant. He is passionate about observing and drawing the natural world.
