Ecological information does not only come from field surveys and specimen collections. Historical art can also preserve observations of the natural world and offer a different window into past biodiversity.

This study examines bird diversity represented in paintings from the Song dynasty (960–1279), connecting cultural records with questions in ecology and natural history.

The paper was published in *Ecology* in 2025.
